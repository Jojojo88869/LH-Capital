The Problem

Your money deserves more than a guess.

Financial markets have never been more accessible—and yet they have never been more confusing.

Investors face conflicting advice, market noise, emotional decisions, scams, poor risk management and financial products they may not fully understand.

For many people, financial losses represent much more than money. They represent years of work, family security, dreams and peace of mind.

LH Capital was created around a simple belief: finance should be built around clarity, trust and responsible decision-making.

Founder & Leadership

Samor Joseph

Samor Joseph is an investment and wealth management professional specialising in investment advisory, portfolio strategy, market intelligence, and wealth preservation — a journey he has pursued since 2018. His approach blends disciplined risk management with global diversification and tactical market opportunities. He is the founder of LH Capital, a private investment consulting firm based in Dubai.

Outside of markets, Samor is a professional athlete, triathlete, and a biker — a discipline that shapes his approach to wealth the same way it shapes his approach to endurance sport: structure over impulse, consistency over intensity, and the long game over the quick win.
